Behind the numbers

How we did the study

We used GTA VI fan discussions, Google Trends, and restaurant locations to compare US markets and fast-food chains.

How did we choose the fast-food chains?

We screened 7,262 comments from discussions specifically about GTA VI and looked for restaurant mentions. From those conversations, we identified 19 fast-food chains to track. Not all 7,262 comments were about food, and a mention does not always mean someone plans to order.

How did we choose and group the cities?

To compare purchase intent, we used the Google Trends search query “buy GTA 6”. Of 210 regions examined, 200 had a search-interest score above 20. We removed one overlapping area so its restaurant locations would not be counted twice. That left 199 areas: 55 with at least 1 million people and 144 with fewer. City names identify each area, but the restaurant counts generally cover its wider metro or local area, not just the city limits. Google Trends scores measure relative interest from 0 to 100, not the number of gamers or searches.

How did we rank cities?

The Readiness Score out of 100 combines GTA VI purchase-search interest (50%) and fast-food outlets per 100,000 residents (50%). Google Trends already uses a 0–100 scale. To make restaurant availability comparable, we give each market a 0–100 score based on how its outlets per resident compare with the other 199 study areas. The two numbers are averaged to produce the Readiness Score. We rank big-city markets separately from smaller markets, but use the same scoring method for everyone. Top-three local fast-food strongholds are shown for context and do not affect the Readiness Score or city rankings. Restaurant location data came from Overture. Across the 199 areas, the average is 27.4 tracked outlets per 100,000 people—an average for this study, not for every US community.

How did we rank fast-food chains?

For each market, we compared a chain’s share of the 19 tracked restaurant locations with its share across the wider US areas studied. We kept markets where it ranked in the top three for that local difference. Each market adds more points when the chain stands out more and GTA VI purchase-search interest is higher. Add all the market points to get the Launch-Night Serving Score. This measures restaurant positioning—not how many meals a chain will actually sell.
